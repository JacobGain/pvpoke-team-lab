# Release v1.1.1

## Mega formats and shared inventory
- Added Mega variants for Great, Ultra, and Master League, with Mega CP derived from owned IVs and level.
- Shared owned Pokémon across leagues while keeping saved teams specific to their format.
- Improved inventory league defaults and added filters for eligible builds and all owned records.

## Simulation and recommendations
- Added Top 100 and Top 250 targets to team simulations.
- Increased recommendation output to as many as ten teams and refined ready-now/planned selection.
- Added replay controls that start when a simulation finishes.

## Current data and responsive layout
- Refreshed PvPoke data and bundled open and Mega ranking groups.
- Adjusted dashboard and mobile spacing and moved advanced battle settings into a collapsible section.
